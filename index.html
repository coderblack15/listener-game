How to play and card index

Goal: reach 10 Resonance before Storm Instability reaches 10 or your Health hits 0.

Each turn you can play up to 3 cards. Click a card to play it, or press keys 1 to 7. Then press End Turn (or E), and the Storm acts.

Tone matters. Pick a tone with the buttons above. Playing a card that matches your tone gives +1 extra on any Resonance it grants, as long as Paradox is 0. Playing a card that does not match costs 1 Resonance.

The Storm can raise Instability, add Paradox, hit you for 2 (Shield absorbs it first), or grow stronger. At Intensity 2 or more it acts twice a turn.
