# Prophecy of the Listener: Relicsong Card Game

By Adrian Blackshire

A small card game you play in your web browser. You are the Listener, singing against the Resonance Storm. Reach 10 Resonance before the Storm's Instability reaches 10 or your Health runs out.

**Play it here:** https://coderblack15.github.io/listener-game/

**New to the game?** Read the [rules for beginners](https://coderblack15.github.io/listener-game/rules.html).

## How it works

- Each turn you can play up to 3 cards, then the Storm takes its turn.
- Every card has a tone: Calm, Empathetic, Reflective, Curious or Vulnerable. Cards that match your chosen tone give a bonus, and cards that don't match cost you.
- Shield blocks the Storm's hits, and Paradox stops your tone bonus, so keep an eye on both.
- The Storm warns you of its next moves. Play a card of the right tone to answer a move and soften it (Counter-song).
- Make all 3 plays in a turn match your tone for a Chorus bonus.
- Attune a Relic to keep its passive effect every turn, and hold one card a turn in your held breath slot.
- Pick a difficulty from Novice to Maelstrom, or switch Mode to Voyage to face three Storms in a row and collect new cards between them.
- The leaderboard only works when the game is opened inside Claude. On GitHub Pages it shows a note instead.
