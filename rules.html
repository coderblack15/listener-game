Prophecy of the Listener · Relicsong Card Game

How to Play

A short guide for your first game. You can learn it in five minutes.

Open the game

The goal

You are the Listener, singing against the Resonance Storm. It is a race.

You win when10 Resonance

Your song is strong enough to calm the sea.

You lose when10 Instability

Or when your Health drops to 0.

Your numbers

Resonance
Your score. Get it to 10 to win.
Health
You start with 10. If it reaches 0, you lose.
Shield
Blocks damage before it hurts your Health. 1 Shield stops 1 damage.
Instability
How wild the Storm is. If it reaches 10, you lose.
Paradox
A bad thing. While it is above 0, matching your tone gives no bonus. It drops by 1 at the end of each of your turns.
Intensity
How strong the Storm is. Once it is 2 or more, the Storm acts twice each turn.

Your turn, step by step

  1. Pick a tone. Tap one of the five tone buttons. Cards in your hand that match it will glow.
  2. Play up to 3 cards. Tap a card to play it. The three dots show how many plays you have left. You don't have to use all three.
  3. Press End Turn. Your hand fills back up to 5 cards and Paradox drops by 1.
  4. The Storm takes its turn. It does something bad (see below). Then it's your turn again.

Tones: the one trick to learn

Every card has a tone. You have one active tone at a time, and you can change it whenever you like, for free.

CalmEmpatheticReflectiveCuriousVulnerable
  • Play a card that matches your tone, and any Resonance it gives you gets +1 extra. This only works when Paradox is 0.
  • Play a card that does not match, and you lose 1 Resonance.
Example. Your tone is Calm and you play Breath of Tide, which is a Calm card that gives 2 Resonance. It matches, so you get 3. If your tone had been Curious, you would get 2 and then lose 1, so only 1.

Simple habit: before you tap a card, set your tone to that card's tone. Each card tells you if it matches.

How to win, step by step

  1. Build Resonance every turn. Breath of Tide, Drift of Memory, Tide-Bloom Pendant, Breath-Shell and Resonance Surge all add to it. Get to 10 and you win.
  2. Match your tone first. A matching card gives +1 extra Resonance. A card that doesn’t match costs you 1.
  3. Keep the Storm in check. When Instability climbs past 5, spend a play lowering it with Listener’s Echo, the Archive Shard or Calm the Tempest.
  4. Protect your Health. Use Shield cards when Health is getting low, so the Storm’s hits bounce off.
  5. Draw when your hand is weak. Bloom of Light and Drift-Spiral Charm bring in new cards to find what you need.

Tips for your first game

  • Always match your tone before you play. It is the easiest +1 in the game.
  • Keep an eye on Instability. If it reaches 6 or 7, play Listener’s Echo, the Archive Shard or Calm the Tempest.
  • Save Calm the Tempest for a bad moment. It also stops the Storm from acting that turn.
  • Play Pulse of Crystal early if your Health is low. Shield soaks up the Storm’s hits.
  • Try to keep Paradox at 0. Breath-Shell removes it; Glyph Cascade adds it.
