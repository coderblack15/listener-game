Prophecy of the Listener · Relicsong Card Game

How to Play

A short guide for your first game. You can learn it in five minutes.

Open the game

The goal

You are the Listener, singing against the Resonance Storm. It is a race.

You win when10 Resonance

Your song is strong enough to calm the sea.

You lose when10 Instability

Or when your Health drops to 0.

Your numbers

Resonance
Your progress. Get it to 10 to win.
Health
You start with 10. If it reaches 0, you lose.
Shield
Blocks damage before it hurts your Health. 1 Shield stops 1 damage.
Instability
How wild the Storm is. If it reaches 10, you lose.
Paradox
A bad thing. While it is above 0, matching your tone gives no bonus and you can't sing a Chorus. It drops by 1 at the end of each of your turns.
Intensity
How strong the Storm is. Once it is 2 or more, the Storm makes two moves each turn.

Your turn, step by step

  1. Read the Storm's warning. The red strip near the top shows exactly what the Storm will do when your turn ends, and which tone answers each move. Plan around it.
  2. Pick a tone. Tap one of the five tone buttons. Cards in your hand that match it will glow. Your first change each turn is free.
  3. Play up to 3 cards. Tap a card to play it. The three dots show how many plays you have left. You don't have to use all three.
  4. Hold a card if you like. Once a turn, press Hold a card (or H) and pick one to save for later. Holding doesn't use a play.
  5. Press End Turn. Your hand fills back up to 5 cards and Paradox drops by 1.
  6. The Storm makes its moves. It does what the warning said, minus anything you answered. Then it shows its next moves, and it's your turn again.

Tones: the one trick to learn

Every card has a tone. You have one active tone at a time.

CalmEmpatheticReflectiveCuriousVulnerable
  • Play a card that matches your tone, and any Resonance it gives you gets +1 extra. This only works when Paradox is 0.
  • Play a card that does not match, and you lose 1 Resonance.
  • Your first tone change each turn is free. Every extra change costs 1 Resonance. If you have no Resonance to pay, you take 1 Paradox instead.
Example. Your tone is Calm and you play Breath of Tide, which is a Calm card that gives 2 Resonance. It matches, so you get 3. If your tone had been Curious, you would get 2 and then lose 1, so only 1.

Simple habit: look at your hand, pick the tone that most of your best cards share, and stick with it for the turn. Switching back and forth will drain your Resonance.

Counter-song New

Each tone can answer one of the Storm's moves. When you play a card that matches your tone while that move is in the red strip, the move is softened. The chip turns green so you can see it worked.

One card answers one move. Silence and Surge have no tone that answers them. A card in your hand that would answer a move shows an Answers tag.

Chorus New

Make all 3 of your plays in a turn match your tone while Paradox is 0, and you sing a Chorus. You then pick a reward: +1 Resonance or −1 Instability. The line next to your turn counter tracks how close you are.

Example. Your tone is Empathetic. You play Pulse of Crystal, Crystal Mask Fragment and Resonance Surge. All three match, so you get a Chorus. The first one also answered a warned Strike.

Attuned Relics New

After you play a Relic, the game asks if you want to attune it. An attuned Relic sits in your Relic slot instead of going to the discard, and its passive effect works every turn. You have one slot, so attuning a new Relic sends the old one to your discard.

A Fracture shatters your attuned Relic. Answer Fractures with a Calm card to protect it.

Held breath New

Once per turn you can set one card aside in the held breath slot. Press Hold a card (or H), then tap the card. It stays there until you tap it to play it, on any later turn.

  • Drown can't take a held card.
  • A held card doesn't count toward your 5-card hand, so you still draw back up to 5.
  • Playing it uses one of your plays, and the usual tone rules apply.

How to win, step by step

  1. Build Resonance every turn. Breath of Tide, Drift of Memory, Tide-Bloom Pendant, Breath-Shell and Resonance Surge all add to it. Get to 10 and you win.
  2. Pick one tone and commit. A matching card gives +1 extra Resonance, and three matching plays make a Chorus. A card that doesn’t match costs you 1, and so does every tone change after your first.
  3. Answer the warning. If a Strike is coming, play Shield or an Empathetic card. If a Swell is coming and Instability is already high, lower it now or answer it with a Vulnerable card.
  4. Keep the Storm in check. When Instability climbs past 5, spend a play lowering it with Listener’s Echo, the Archive Shard or Calm the Tempest.
  5. Finish before turn 8. After that, the rising tide adds Instability every turn.

Scores and the leaderboard

When a game ends, you get a score and can save it under any name you choose. The leaderboard shows the top 10, and each player keeps only their best.

A win
1000 points, plus 40 for every turn under 25, plus 25 for each Health and 10 for each Shield you have left.
A loss
50 points for each Resonance you reached, plus 10 for each turn you survived.
A Voyage
Each calmed Storm is worth 800, plus 30 for every turn under 15 in that Storm. Finishing all three adds 25 for each Health and 10 for each Shield left. If you fall, you keep the points for Storms already calmed, plus 50 per Resonance and 10 per turn survived.

The total is then multiplied by your difficulty, so a Maelstrom win counts double. Fast wins with Health to spare on hard levels score the most. Saving needs you to be signed in to Claude with permission to save on this game.

Tips for your first game

  • Read the red strip before every turn. It is the most useful thing on the screen, and it tells you which tone answers each move.
  • Treat your free tone change as precious. Discord will try to steal it, unless you answer it with a Reflective card.
  • When Drown is coming, play your best cards first or hold the one you can't lose.
  • When Silence is coming, make this turn count. Next turn you'll only have 2 plays.
  • Save Calm the Tempest for a bad moment, like two big moves coming at once.
  • Try to keep Paradox at 0. Breath-Shell removes it; Glyph Cascade and Fracture add it. Paradox also blocks your Chorus.
  • Attune a Relic early. Its effect pays off every turn it survives.
